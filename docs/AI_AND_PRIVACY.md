# AI, Privacy, and Human Oversight

Jerbs uses AI as an evidence-grounded research and drafting assistant. It is not an autonomous application system.

## Core rules

- Recommendations should be traceable to job-source or candidate evidence.
- Missing evidence should be identified rather than invented.
- Generated application materials require human review and approval.
- Fit assessments should separate explicit requirements, inference, and uncertainty.
- No application is submitted automatically.
- Sensitive candidate information remains outside this public repository.

## Private data boundary

Jerbs maintains a strict separation between public product documentation and the private working environment. The following are never intended for this repository:

- evidence-vault contents;
- résumés and application materials containing personal information;
- email and correspondence;
- private contact information;
- databases and working exports;
- backups and operational logs;
- credentials, tokens, and private configuration;
- proprietary implementation and evaluation logic.

## Responsible evaluation

AI may assist with interpreting job requirements and comparing them with verified career evidence. It should not silently reject opportunities or exaggerate qualifications. Near matches, conflicting evidence, expired listings, and uncertain source data should remain visible for human judgment.
