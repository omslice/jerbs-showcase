# Jerbs

**Private, local-first career intelligence—with evidence behind every recommendation.**

Jerbs is an actively developed system for discovering opportunities, organizing career evidence, evaluating fit, preparing application materials, and tracking the full job-search process without surrendering control of sensitive personal data.

> **Status:** Private alpha. This repository is a public product showcase and development log. The application source, evidence vault, résumés, correspondence, databases, exports, backups, and logs are not published here.

## Why Jerbs exists

Traditional job boards optimize for listings and clicks. A serious search requires something different: broader source discovery, reliable evidence, transparent evaluation, tailored materials, and persistent knowledge about the candidate and each opportunity.

Jerbs is being built as a career-intelligence operating system rather than an automatic application bot.

## Product workflow

1. **Build an evidence-backed profile** from approved career materials.
2. **Discover opportunities** across supported job sources and targeted research.
3. **Normalize and track listings** without losing provenance or lifecycle history.
4. **Evaluate fit** using explicit requirements, preferences, evidence, and uncertainty.
5. **Prepare tailored materials** grounded in verified experience.
6. **Manage the pipeline** with statuses, reminders, decisions, and follow-up.
7. **Learn from outcomes** while preserving a reviewable audit trail.

## How AI is used

| AI-assisted capability | Contribution | Human control |
|---|---|---|
| Research planning | Suggests targeted searches and sources beyond generic job boards | Users choose scope and sources |
| Opportunity analysis | Extracts requirements, responsibilities, constraints, and signals | Original postings and provenance remain reviewable |
| Evidence-backed matching | Compares roles with verified candidate evidence | Unsupported claims are not added |
| Gap and uncertainty analysis | Identifies missing evidence, ambiguity, and possible mismatches | Users decide how to interpret or resolve gaps |
| Material preparation | Drafts tailored résumés, cover letters, and supporting narratives | Users review and approve every document |
| Search retrospectives | Finds patterns in results, near misses, and pipeline outcomes | Users decide which strategies to change |

AI recommendations are not treated as facts. Jerbs is designed to show why a conclusion was reached and what evidence supports it.

## What Jerbs does not do

- It does not automatically submit job applications.
- It does not invent experience or qualifications.
- It does not publish or sync the private evidence vault here.
- It does not expose private résumés, emails, contact details, databases, logs, or exports.
- It does not turn an AI score into an irreversible decision.

## Privacy architecture

The public showcase is deliberately separated from the private working system. Sensitive candidate materials live outside this repository. Public documentation describes product intent and development progress without exposing proprietary implementation or personal records.

Read [AI, Privacy, and Human Oversight](docs/AI_AND_PRIVACY.md) for the operating principles.
